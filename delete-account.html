Delete your Forja account

You can permanently delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Forja and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm.

Optional: before deleting, tap Download my data on the same screen to keep a copy of your workouts.

By email

If you can’t open the app, write to vladbaracut7@yahoo.com from the email address of your account and ask for it to be deleted. We do it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, workouts, routines, programs, body weight and measurements, and your settings are removed permanently from our servers. Nothing is kept. A Google Play subscription must be cancelled separately in Google Play.
